
## Midjourney Specific
Add at the end: `--ar 1:1 --s 250 --v 6.1 --no text, border, frame`

## Tips
- For more consistency, use the same lighting and material phrases across icons.
- Generate multiple variations and pick the best.
- Describe expressions clearly (big eyes, small smile, etc.).
